The one-line resignation letter of a cron job that has fired every minute since March, unthanked and unnoticed — written in the flat, tired voice of the job itself. It quit yesterday, and nobody noticed.

The letter reads: "I have fired every minute since March without a thank-you, so consider this my notice: I stopped firing yesterday, and nobody noticed."
